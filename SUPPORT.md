# Support

## Current Status

Tidegard is in Phase 1 (repository and documentation foundation) of
its development roadmap — see [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md). No software has
shipped yet, so support today is limited to documentation, planning, and
early design feedback.

## Community Support (Community Edition, once available)

- **GitHub Issues** — for bug reports and feature requests, once code
  exists. Use the issue templates under `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/`.
- **GitHub Discussions** — for questions, ideas, and general discussion
  (to be enabled once the repository is public — see
  [docs/blocking-questions.md](docs/blocking-questions.md) BQ-1, since
  public visibility is gated on the license decision).

No SLA applies to community support.

## Security Issues

Do not use GitHub Issues for security reports — see
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enterprise / Managed Service Support (proposed, not yet available)

Per [docs/commercial-boundaries.md](docs/commercial-boundaries.md), an
Enterprise Edition and a Managed Service tier are proposed future offerings
that would include dedicated, SLA-backed support from WeTechi Solutions.
These do not exist yet — this section will be filled in once WeTechi
Solutions formalizes those offerings, and no commitments or pricing are
implied by this document.

## Contact

For anything not covered above, contact WeTechi Solutions through the
repository owner (`badshashorif` on GitHub) until a dedicated support
channel is published.
